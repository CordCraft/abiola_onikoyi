Project · Prototype stage · Started November 2026

The watchman for your shop.

One wall box that cuts a Nigerian shop's diesel bill, stops cash and fuel leaking, keeps tax records ready and turns its data into a credit profile. Owners get instant Telegram alerts with photos, a daily report and a simple dashboard.

Download the playbook (PDF) Open 3D full screen

Power

Knows when grid power and the generator are on, flags a generator left running while grid is back, and measures every kWh.

Diesel

An ultrasonic sensor in the tank cap catches siphoning and confirms every refuel.

Security

Door, motion and tamper sensors with a camera that sends a photo the moment something happens after hours.

Cash and records

A two-minute end-of-day cash count shows any gap, and the data builds tax records and a credit profile.
